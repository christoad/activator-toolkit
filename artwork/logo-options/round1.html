Activator Toolkit: Logo Options

Six ideas built around radio and the contact map, drawn in the same thin-line style as the current logo so the brand stays familiar. Each shows a large version plus the small sizes WordPress uses (128px plugin icon, 48px, 24px) on light and dark backgrounds.

Current logo (SOTA only), for comparison.

1. Contact Web

Your station (red, with signal rings) at the center of lines to the stations you worked, on a faint globe grid. Basically the contact map as an icon. Works for SOTA and POTA equally.

2. Summit + Park

The current mountain kept as is, with a green pine tree beside it for POTA. Closest to the existing look; reads clearly as "SOTA & POTA".

3. Antenna Tower

A lattice antenna mast sending out radio waves. Strongly "ham radio", neutral between summits and parks.

4. Globe Arcs

A globe with dotted great-circle paths from your station out to distant contacts. Emphasizes distance and DX.

5. Signal Pin

A map pin that is also broadcasting, with dotted links to nearby contacts. Map plus radio in one shape; the pin stays readable at tiny sizes.

6. Handheld Radio

A handheld (HT) transmitting, with lines out to contact dots. The most literal "radio" option; feels portable, like an activation.
