Activator Toolkit: Logo Options, Round 2

Options 1 and 5 with a topographic map behind them instead of latitude and longitude lines. Two takes on each. See round 1.

1A. Contact Web on Topo

Same contact web as before, now over contour lines of a hill and the slopes around it.

1B. Station on the Hilltop

The red station sits right at the top of the hill's contour rings, so it reads as "operating from a summit or park", with contacts radiating out.

5A. Signal Pin on Topo

The outlined pin with radio waves, sitting on the topo map with dotted links to contacts. The pin has a light fill so the contours don't run through it.

5B. Solid Red Pin

Same idea with a solid red pin and white radio waves. Bolder, and the easiest of the four to recognize at tiny sizes.
