Activator Toolkit: Logo Options, Round 3

Options 1 and 5 over a real topographic map: the contour lines are traced from actual elevation data for Mt. Diablo, California, with a bolder index line every fifth contour, just like a USGS topo. The summit sits under the station or pin in each version. Earlier rounds: 1, 2.

1A. Contact Web on Topo

Same contact web as before, now over real contour lines, with the station near the summit.

1B. Station on the Hilltop

The red station sits right on the summit, so it reads as "operating from a summit or park", with contacts radiating out.

5A. Signal Pin on Topo

The outlined pin with radio waves, sitting on the topo map with dotted links to contacts. The pin has a light fill so the contours don't run through it.

5B. Solid Red Pin

Same idea with a solid red pin and white radio waves. Bolder, and the easiest of the four to recognize at tiny sizes.
